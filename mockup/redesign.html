What it takes to build

  • Rail and bottom bar. TabBar becomes both, and layout.tsx becomes a two-column shell. The Week summary's side-by-side breakpoint moves from 1310px to about 1380px.
  • Week dropdown. A new picker on the roster header, fed from the same query as History.
  • Staff. StaffTable splits into a list and an edit panel opened by ?person=. LeaveTable becomes the timeline and a Book leave form.
  • Settings. A section component and a scroll-aware index. TradingHoursTable becomes the strip, and TemplatesTable becomes the bars with an edit row.
  • Highlight. One shared style for the tint, border and text, used by every current or selected state and by the shift chips.
  • Phone fix. The sideways scroll from the first review comes with this work.
