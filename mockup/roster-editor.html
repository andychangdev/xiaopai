Staff

Rows appear on the roster in this order — use ↑↓ to arrange them the way you think about your staff. Everyone active appears on every week's roster. Available is the weekdays a person can normally work — rostering them outside it raises a warning but is never blocked. Deactivating someone takes them off new weeks but leaves them on rosters you've already published.

People

Booked leave

A leave period blocks those days on the roster — shifts can't be added while someone is away. It spans as many weeks as you need and is never touched by Clear week.

Expected weekly hours is optional — when set, the roster highlights anyone more than 20% above or below it. Availability is the recurring pattern; one-off leave is an Away marker on the roster itself.

Settings

Trading hours and shift templates — the things that hold from week to week. Closing a day is done on the roster itself, one week at a time, so a public holiday never leaks into another week.

Weeks
